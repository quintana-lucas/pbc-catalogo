# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: neighbors of Planta Baja Cocina (PBC) who order home-style food for the week. They open the site on a phone, often from a WhatsApp or Instagram link, browse the weekly menu and categories (tartas, postres, panadería, sopitas, platos premium al vacío), add items to a cart and send the order by WhatsApp.

Secondary: people planning an event who want a catering quote. They use the cotizador (guests, options, extras, date, zone) and confirm by WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

A catalog for PBC, a small home-cooking business, that lets customers choose what they want and hand the order to the owner as a ready-written WhatsApp message. Success is a customer sending a complete, clear order or quote request without back-and-forth.

## Positioning

Not a store with checkout: an order-by-WhatsApp catalog where the conversation with the cook stays the point of sale (delivery is coordinated there). Product data and catering prices come straight from the owner's Google Sheets.

## Operating Context

- The owner updates products and catering prices in Google Sheets published as CSV. A GitHub Action rebuilds the static site on every push, on demand, and hourly from 08 to 22 (Argentina time) when the sheet changed.
- Publishing is guarded: a broken or much smaller sheet does not replace the live site.
- Hosted on GitHub Pages. Currently no custom domain (`dominio` is empty, `indexar: false`).
- Spanish (Argentina), prices in pesos.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- WhatsApp is the only checkout: no payments, accounts, server or database. Every order and quote ends as a pre-written WhatsApp message.
- The owner is non-technical and edits only the sheets and `config.json`; the site must keep rebuilding without code changes.
- Mobile-first: phone use is the default case.
- Static output generated by `build.py` from `plantilla/`; client-specific values live in `config.json`.
- Product states: empty price shows "Consultar", `Disponible = no` shows "Sin stock"; a missing photo falls back to an emoji.
- Catering module is opt-in (`pedidos_especiales.activo`); its UI name is "Catering" while the config key stays `pedidos_especiales`.
- Planned (design agreed, not built): a landing page with two entries, Catálogo and Catering, per-module header navigation, and the catering quote presented as a paper comanda (`docs/diseno-acceso-catering.md`).
- Undecided: where the catalog lives once the landing page takes `/` (existing shared URLs must not break), and where «¿Quiénes somos?» goes if it is re-enabled (currently hidden).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Planta Baja Cocina (PBC). Tagline: «Hecho con amor». Voice: warm, home-made, informal Argentine Spanish (voseo).
- Established identity is fixed and not up for replacement: the logo (`assets/logo.png`) and the existing palette (burgundy, olive, gold on warm cream) configured in `config.json`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real catalog data: `data/productos.csv` and the published product sheet (24 products in 6 categories at last build).
- Catering options and extras: `data/catering.csv` and the published catering sheet.
- Assets in `assets/` (logo, favicons, share image). Product photos in `img/` (currently only a `.gitkeep` is tracked, so products show emoji).
- Not on hand: testimonials, customer counts, press. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. The order message is the product: whatever the visitor does should end in a complete, clear WhatsApp message.
2. The owner's sheet is the source of truth; design must hold up with whatever content the owner publishes, including missing photos, empty prices and sold-out items.
3. Phone first, one hand, often on a slow connection.
4. Catálogo (everyday meals) and Catering (events) are separate needs; the visitor chooses, the site doesn't push one onto the other.
5. Warmth comes from the cook's voice and the existing identity, not from extra decoration.
